# Decisions

- The React app in this repo is the desktop prototype only.
- Editor topics each have their own note: header, export, library, preview, inspector, timeline, ruler, playhead, and time. `docs/desktop-layout.md` is the index.
- The phone UI will be a separate React app. It does not live here.
- The shipping desktop UI will be Rust, eframe, and egui. This React UI is the picture, not the port.
- The editor stack is head, then library / preview / inspector, then timeline.
- The command row is out of the header until it has a place. `CommandBox` stays in the project.
- A dark gap separates the panels. Dragging happens in that gap. The timeline grows upward and stops before it covers the preview or the transport. The side panels grow sideways. The preview takes the rest.
- The chrome is gray. Regions separate by surface color. Borders are not the layout. Inside the library, a hairline splits the icon rail from the assets. Inside the timeline, a hairline splits the toolbar from the ruler.
- A slider is a thin track and a plain white thumb. It has no border and no focus outline. A color grade may paint the track.
- Hovering a tool, a clip, a marker, a library card, or an icon button shows its name. A clip also shows its time span.
- Editor text does not select. Fields still do.
- The library, preview, inspector, and timeline share `#1c1c1c`. The project bar stays `#262626`.
- Export opens a dialog. Resolution runs from 144p to 8K. Format chooses the codec list: MP4 is H.264 or H.265, MOV adds ProRes, WebM is VP9 or AV1. Audio follows the format. The cover is the vows frame. Export sections are Video, Audio, GIF, and Caption. The name folds the fields. The switch turns that part on or off. GIF and Caption start off and folded. The dialog does not write a video file.
- Media can show the project bin or a free-stock shelf. A click selects a card. Shift selects the range from the last click. Command toggles a card. A drag across empty space in the media grid selects the cards inside the box, and Shift keeps the cards already selected. A click on empty space clears the selection. Double-clicking a stock shot adds it to a video track. The prototype does not download footage.
- A short white toast drops a short way from the timeline toolbar, then fades out where it lands. It reports a still, locking or unlocking a lane, and other edits that do not land.
- Right-click empty space in the project bin for a new folder or a sort. A folder or a file has the menu from the reference. Export from the bin does not write a file. Drag a media card onto a folder, or onto a place in the path, to move it. The same drag still places the card on the timeline.
- Transcribe places caption lines on a text track. It does not hear the clip. A filter, an effect, an adjustment, or a video grade tints the mock frame. Volume moves a meter. The prototype still does not render video or play a file.
- A text clip opens the text inspector. Audio, stickers, effects, filters, and adjustments each open their own inspector. Text Bubble, Effects, and Tracking are filled in. Adjust includes HSL, curves, color wheels, and a mask. The tabs are Text, Animation, Tracking, and Text to speech. Bubble and a text effect show on the title. The panel does not generate speech, follow a subject, or save a preset.
- A library card stays 144 by 96, in one column or several. The grid shows one column until another card fits, then two, then three, and so on. The swatch fills the card, and the name sits on it. Dragging a card carries that same picture, with no plate behind it. A selected card has a white edge around the whole card. The plus on a card adds that clip at the end of the matching lane. A card already on the timeline says “On timeline”.
- Right-click a clip to open its menu. Video lists the picture actions. Other kinds list copy, cut, delete, and a few actions for that kind. Delete removes the clip. The prototype does not write a file.
- The transport keeps timecode at the left and play in the center. The readout is `HH:MM:SS:FF`. Drop frame uses `HH:MM:SS;FF`. A camera at the right marks a still of the current frame. The prototype does not write that file. The skip buttons are gone.
- The timeline has no title bar. Time stays on the transport. The toolbar sits above the ruler and holds undo, redo, select, split, delete, freeze, reverse, mirror, rotate, crop, transcribe, split scenes, and snap. Undo and redo step through edits to the mock timeline. Delete removes the selection. Freeze, reverse, mirror, rotate, crop, transcribe, and split scenes are on for a video clip. Delete is on for every clip. Reverse and transcribe are also on for audio. Mirror and rotate are also on for text, stickers, and compositions. Crop is also on for a composition. Freeze and split scenes stay on video. Freeze, reverse, mirror, rotate, crop, and split scenes do not change the mock. Transcribe places the captions. The playhead mark sits on the tick row, as tall as a major tick, and the line runs through the tracks. It does not cross the toolbar, the track head, or the cover column. The drawn time is the current frame.
- The timeline uses the CapCut 9.6.0 track kinds in `docs/capcut-desktop-re.md`. Adjust is its own kind. Templates are presets. Transitions are not tracks. One video track is the main layer. Overlay video sits above it. Clips drag from the library and move on the timeline. A lane only holds its own kind. Dropping a clip on another kind inserts a new lane there. The main track is magnetic. Its lane stays when the last clip is removed.
- Magnet, linkage, trim, zoom, and markers follow the CapCut timeline notes. Magnet can be turned off. Linkage makes a clip that sits on one main clip follow it. A clip edge trims media in or out. A transition sits on a main-track cut, and split refuses inside it. Markers and beat marks are timeline tools.
- Snapping draws a vertical line and can land on markers, beats, transition cuts, reference lines, and the ends of the timeline. Reference lines are pulled off the ruler and stay. A drag near the side scrolls the sheet. The playhead can be dragged. Zoom is pixels per second. The zoom-out limit is a quarter of the lanes and moves with the cut. Zoom to fit uses three quarters of the lanes. A locked track refuses edits, and a locked main track refuses the magnet.
- The main lane is the bottom picture lane. Only audio sits below it. Above it, from the top: plugin effect, text and stickers in one band, effect, filter, adjust, then overlay video. A drop on the wrong kind inserts the lane in that band.
- A new clip lands where it is dropped when that time is free, including on Main. Empty space opens a new lane in that kind's band. An occupied time is refused. The first clip sets the scale so the cut fills three quarters of the lanes. Another clip keeps that scale. With the magnet on, a drop on Main packs into that lane. A drop off Main stays where it is placed.
- A dragged clip follows the pointer until it is released. The timeline does not move that clip before the drop.
- The timeline starts with no clips. The only lane is Main. Library cards are placeholders. Dragging one onto the timeline places it. With no clips, split, marker, beat, play, the still, zoom, and the playhead are off.
- Main is always on the timeline. It sits in the vertical center. Other lanes spread up and down from it with an even gap.
- The timeline column is toolbar, ruler, then the tracks. The track head is 130px. A 72px column between the head and the lanes is reserved for cover art. Each lane fills the remaining width at every zoom. Clips are arranged on the lanes.
- A trackpad pinch zooms the timeline. The time under the pointer stays where it is. Zoom in doubles the scale and zoom out halves it, down to the moving floor and up to the two-frame step.
- The ruler uses the CapCut 9.6.0 step table at the project frame rate. It starts at 30 fps. Labels are clock time, `MM:SS` under an hour and `HH:MM:SS` from an hour. A frame step inside a second shows the frame offset, such as `15f`. Clip times stay in seconds. The ruler fills the lanes at every zoom. Past the end of the cut, the ticks keep going.
- Drop frame is a project setting for 29.97 and 59.94. The ruler then labels every major tick `HH:MM:SS;FF`. 29.97 skips frame numbers 0 and 1 at the start of each minute. 59.94 skips 0 through 3. Every tenth minute skips none. Other rates stay on the clock labels. Choosing 29.97 or 59.94 turns drop frame on.
- With nothing selected, the inspector shows the project. A selected video clip opens the clip panel: Video, Audio, Speed, Animation, Adjust, and AI stylize. Video Basic holds position and size, then the effect rows. Audio Basic holds volume and fades. Animation lists In, Out, and Combo. Each slot holds one placeholder or none, and duration starts at 2 seconds. Adjust Basic holds LUT and the color, lightness, and effect sliders. The controls are a picture and do not change the frame. Modify opens a dialog in the export chrome. It sets the name, import, color space, free layer, proxy, ratio, resolution, frame rate, and drop frame. Free layer cannot be turned off once it is on. Save keeps the settings in the mock project and does not write a file. The frame rate is the sequence rate the ruler uses.
- Shift-click adds or removes a clip. A click on empty space clears the selection. A drag across empty timeline space selects the clips inside the box. Delete removes every selected clip, including the last clip on Main. The Main lane stays.
- Icons are Hugeicons free icons. The blade mark stays custom.
- Tailwind is allowed and not currently used.
- This prototype lives on the `prototype` branch of `1mrnewton/cutlass`. `ui-design` is the other checkout. Do not push over `main` or the other branches already in that repo.
