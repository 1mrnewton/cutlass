# Export

Export is on the right of the project bar and opens a dialog. The prototype does not write a file.

A portrait cover sits on the left. The settings stack in one column beside it, each as a dropdown. The dialog is a fixed size. Its title bar stays put, like a Mac dialog, and the settings scroll inside.

The sections are Video, Audio, GIF, and Caption, with a wide gap between them. The section name folds and unfolds its fields. The switch turns that part of the export on or off. GIF and Caption start off and folded.

Video holds resolution, frame rate, format, codec, quality, rate control, and bitrate. Resolution runs from 144p to 8K. Format chooses the codec list: MP4 is H.264 or H.265, MOV adds ProRes, WebM is VP9 or AV1. ProRes swaps bitrate for a profile. Audio holds codec, sample rate, channels, and bitrate, and follows the format. GIF holds frame rate and loop. Caption holds burn-in or SRT.

The export name starts as the project name and stays independent of a later rename.
