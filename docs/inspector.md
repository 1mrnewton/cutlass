# Inspector

With a video clip selected, the inspector is the clip panel. Video, Audio, Speed, Animation, Adjust, and AI stylize are the tabs. Video opens on Basic: position and size, then the effect rows. Audio opens on volume and fades. Animation opens on In, Out, and Combo. Each slot holds one animation or none. Search filters that list. Duration is the length of the chosen animation and starts at 2 seconds. The thumbnails are placeholders. Adjust opens on Basic: LUT, then color, lightness, and effect sliders. HSL, Curves, Color wheel, and Mask are part of the picture. Sliders are a thin track and a white thumb, with no outline. Hovering an icon button shows its name. The controls are a picture. They do not change the frame.

With a text clip selected, the inspector is the text panel. Text, Animation, Tracking, and Text to speech are the tabs. Text opens on Basic: the line, font, size, style, case, color, spacing, alignment, preset styles, then position and size. Blend, stroke, background, glow, and shadow fold under that. Bubble and Effects are part of the picture. Animation opens on In, Out, and Loop. The thumbnails are placeholders. Text to speech lists voices and does not generate audio. Tracking is part of the picture. It does not follow a subject. Save as preset does not store one. The controls do not change the frame.

With another clip selected, the inspector shows that clip. Guide and transition selections have their own panels and take priority.

With nothing selected, the inspector shows the project: name, where it would be saved, color space, import, free layer, proxy, ratio, resolution, frame rate, and drop frame when the rate is 29.97 or 59.94. The title is cyan.

Modify opens a dialog in the export chrome. Save keeps those settings on the mock project. The dialog does not write a file. Free layer stays on once it is turned on. Cancel discards the draft, including a free-layer turn-on that was not saved. The frame rate is the sequence rate.
