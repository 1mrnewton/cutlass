# Inspector

With a video clip selected, the inspector is the clip panel. Video, Audio, Speed, Animation, Adjust, and AI stylize are the tabs. Video opens on Basic: position and size, then the effect rows. Audio opens on volume and fades. Animation opens on In, Out, and Combo. Each slot holds one animation or none. Search filters that list. Duration is the length of the chosen animation and starts at 2 seconds. The thumbnails are placeholders. Adjust opens on Basic: LUT, then color, lightness, and effect sliders. HSL, Curves, Color wheel, and Mask are controls on that panel. Sliders are a thin track and a white thumb, with no outline. Hovering an icon button shows its name. The grade tints the mock frame. It does not render the picture.

With a text clip selected, the inspector is the text panel. Text, Animation, Tracking, and Text to speech are the tabs. Text opens on Basic: the line, font, size, style, case, color, spacing, alignment, preset styles, then position and size. Blend, stroke, background, glow, and shadow fold under that. Bubble picks a shape behind the line. Effects picks shadow, glow, or an outline. Animation opens on In, Out, and Loop. The thumbnails are placeholders. Text to speech lists voices and does not generate audio. Tracking picks a face, a body, a hand, or a custom target. It does not follow a subject. Save as preset does not store one.

An audio clip opens volume, fades, denoise, and speed. Volume moves the meter on the frame. The prototype does not play a file. A sticker opens opacity and a short animation list, and the sticker sits on the frame. An effect or a filter opens an amount, and a filter also opens temperature. An adjustment opens Basic, HSL, Curves, Color wheel, and Mask. Those color controls tint the mock frame. They do not render the picture.

Guide and transition selections have their own panels and take priority.

With nothing selected, the inspector shows the project: name, where it would be saved, color space, import, free layer, proxy, ratio, resolution, frame rate, and drop frame when the rate is 29.97 or 59.94. The title is cyan.

Modify opens a dialog in the export chrome. Save keeps those settings on the mock project. The dialog does not write a file. Free layer stays on once it is turned on. Cancel discards the draft, including a free-layer turn-on that was not saved. The frame rate is the sequence rate.
