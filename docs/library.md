# Library

The library is an icon rail and the asset panel, split by a hairline. The hairline is not a drag handle. The rail is media, audio, text, stickers, effects, transitions, filters, adjustments, and templates.

Media has the project bin and a free-stock shelf. Right-click empty space in the bin for a new folder or a sort. A folder or a file has its own menu. Drag a media card onto a folder, or onto a place in the path, to move it there. A library card stays 144 by 96. The swatch fills the card, and the name sits on it. Dragging a card carries that same picture: the swatch, with the name on it, and no plate behind it. Hovering a card shows its name. Hovering a card that can sit on a lane also shows a plus. The plus adds that clip at the end of the matching lane. A card already on the timeline says “On timeline”. The grid adds a column when another card fits, and a single column does not grow the card.

A click selects a media card. The selected card has a white edge around the whole card. Shift selects the range from the last click. Command toggles a card. A drag across empty space in the grid draws a box and selects every card it touches. Shift keeps the cards already selected. A click on empty space clears the selection. A plain click on a folder still opens it. Double-clicking a free-stock shot adds it to a video track. Dragging a card onto the timeline still places it. Nothing is downloaded. Export from the bin does not write a file. Filters and adjustments are their own shelves. Templates are presets, not a track kind. Transitions are not a track kind. Cards are placeholders, not media files.
