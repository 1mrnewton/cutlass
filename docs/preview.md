# Preview

The preview is the frame and the transport. The frame names the shot under the playhead, then the same timecode the transport shows.

Timecode sits at the left of the transport. Play is centered. A camera at the right marks a still of the current frame. Hovering play or the camera shows its name. The prototype does not write that file. The skip buttons are gone. A filter, an effect, an adjustment, or a video grade tints the wash. A sticker sits on the frame. An audio clip draws a meter. Nothing here renders video or plays a file.

The readout is `HH:MM:SS:FF`, or `HH:MM:SS;FF` when drop frame is on. See `docs/time.md`.
